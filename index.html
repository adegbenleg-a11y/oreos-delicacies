Meet Oreofe Abisola

Oreofe Abisola is a passionate pastry chef and culinary artist based in Abeokuta, Ogun State. Renowned across FUNAAB and Abeokuta for her famous signature eggrolls, Oreofe brings artisan craftsmanship, hygienic baking standards, and heartwarming flavor to every single treat.

From bespoke celebration tiered cakes to velvety milky donuts, fudgy brownies, and crunchy jarred delights, OREOS DELICACIES turns everyday moments into sweet, unforgettable celebrations.
