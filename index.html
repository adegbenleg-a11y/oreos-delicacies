Our Baker & Founder

Meet Oreofe Abisola

Welcome to OREOS DELICACIES! I am Oreofe Abisola, an enthusiastic pastry chef devoted to making every celebration extraordinary through the art of baking.

What started with perfecting my signature golden eggroll has blossomed into one of Abeokuta's most cherished confectionery brands. For me, baking is not just about sugar and flour — it's about crafting an unforgettable sensory experience. We hand-select premium ingredients, observe strict kitchen hygiene, and refuse to cut corners.

Whether you are picking up a quick snack on campus, surprising a loved one with our trending Money Box Cake, or ordering crunchy jar snacks for your family, my team and I pour genuine love into every creation.
